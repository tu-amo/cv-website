AI Strategy

AI-Augmented Strategy & Decision Support

Designing AI-supported decision loops — framing, evidence gathering, options generation, tradeoff analysis, and recommendation — that amplify human judgment rather than replace it.

⚙️ What I Do

  • Design the decision loop: frame → evidence → options → tradeoffs → recommendation
  • Identify which parts of the loop AI can accelerate and which require human judgment
  • Build AI-assisted briefing and synthesis workflows for strategic questions
  • Establish verification and challenge protocols to prevent over-reliance on AI outputs
  • Create decision artefacts that are traceable, auditable, and revisable
  • Connect AI outputs to existing knowledge systems for reuse and accumulation

📦 Outputs

  • AI-assisted decision loop designs (per use case)
  • Prompt architecture for strategic analysis tasks
  • Human-in-the-loop checkpoints and verification protocols
  • Strategy briefs and recommendation memos
  • Traceability and audit trail templates

🎯 Where It Helps

  • Strategy teams that want to use AI but lack structured processes for doing so safely
  • Executives who need faster, better-evidenced options — not just AI-generated text
  • Transformation programmes requiring rapid analysis under ambiguity
  • Organisations building AI capability while maintaining decision accountability

🔗 How This Connects to Enterprise Transformation

  • S/4HANA programmes: AI-assisted fit-gap analysis, faster design decisions, traceability from business requirement to architecture choice
  • AI & Automation: the meta-layer — designing how your organisation uses AI to make decisions, not just to execute tasks
  • Cloud Architecture: AI-supported option analysis for cloud strategy, vendor selection, and operating model design
