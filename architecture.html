Legacy (Batch/Siloed) Modern (API/Unified)

Legacy Challenges

  • Batch Dependencies: Stock updates only once per 24 hours via SAP PI files.
  • Siloed Forecasting: Blue Yonder operates on stale data, leading to stock-outs.
  • Disconnected Stores: POS data is stuck in local servers until end-of-day.

Modern Solutions

  • Real-Time APIs: Sales flow instantly to SAP CAR via BTP Integration Suite.
  • Unified Demand: Forecasts use live sales data for accurate replenishment.
  • Mobile First: Staff use Fiori apps to see global inventory in real-time.
